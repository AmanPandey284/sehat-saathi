# 3-minute Sehat Saathi prototype demo

## 0:00–0:25 — Problem
Busy OPDs spend consultation time collecting repetitive history. Patients also carry fragmented paper records. Sehat Saathi moves structured history collection to a self-service kiosk before consultation.

## 0:25–1:15 — Patient journey
Start consultation → consent → choose Abdominal Pain/Fever/Cough → answer by tap or natural text → demonstrate a conditional branch → optionally use microphone → complete history.

Key line: “This is not a generic chatbot. The question engine controls the clinical flow, while patient wording is retained as evidence.”

## 1:15–1:45 — Safety and records
Show a configured safety flag for a concerning response. Add the demo medical report. Show extracted text/entities and explain that image/PDF OCR is intentionally shown as provider-ready rather than falsely claimed as production OCR.

## 1:45–2:35 — Physician review
Open Physician Review. Show:
- physician-ready structured draft
- original patient evidence
- confidence/source
- confirm/edit/reject controls
- possible medication conflict
- medical timeline

Key line: “AI assistance never becomes the final authority; the physician verifies the record.”

## 2:35–3:00 — Differentiation and scale
Show FHIR-ready export and AYUSH mode.

Close with: “Sehat Saathi combines adaptive history-taking, multimodal input, document intelligence, safety flags, provenance and physician verification in one first-mile clinical intake workflow.”

For blind review, keep names, college branding, personal data, passwords and API keys out of the recording.
